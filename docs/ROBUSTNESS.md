### The wait before a kickoff

A half that starts inside an anchor is waiting for its kickoff, as the dead-ball stage sees it. FAQ.md says BAR-ATH's second half is graded from frame 110,808 but kicks off at 113,742, and that those frames are all `D`; the stage finds that kickoff by itself. *Peak speed* is the highest 8 s team speed in the wait, as a multiple of the match's typical live speed (the median over the frames the stage calls live). A wait that reaches live-play speed is a missed kickoff, where the stage tied the half to a later one. Waits of 10 s or more:
